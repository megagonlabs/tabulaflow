Artifact: Revenue by region
Source: sales
SQL: SELECT region, SUM(revenue_usd) AS total_revenue_usd
FROM sales
GROUP BY region
ORDER BY total_revenue_usd DESC;
DataFrame:
   region  total_revenue_usd
0   West               2000
1   East               1500
Artifact: Open high-priority support tickets
Source: support
SQL: SELECT ticket_id, subject, priority, status
FROM support
WHERE lower(priority) = 'high' AND lower(status) != 'resolved'
ORDER BY ticket_id;
DataFrame:
    ticket_id                        subject priority status
0        201      Checkout payment failures     high   open
1        202  Invoice downloads unavailable     high   open
