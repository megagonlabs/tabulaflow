Question: In which city can you find the school in the state of California with the lowest latitude coordinates and what is its lowest grade? Indicate the school name.
Predicted SQL: SELECT s.City, f."Low Grade", s.School
FROM schools s
INNER JOIN frpm f ON s.CDSCode = f.CDSCode
WHERE s.State = 'CA' AND s.Latitude IS NOT NULL
ORDER BY s.Latitude ASC
LIMIT 1;
Query result:
         City Low Grade             School
0  San Ysidro         K  Willow Elementary
