Entities:
  Post - A piece of content in the community (question or answer) with body, title, sc...
  User - A registered community member with profile, reputation, and activity counts.
  Comment - A user-authored remark attached to a post, with text and score.
  Vote - A voting action applied to a post (upvote, downvote, bounty, etc.).
  PostRevision - A historical revision or event for a post capturing text and metadata changes.
  BadgeAward - An instance of a badge being awarded to a user at a point in time.
  Tag - A topic label with usage count and optional wiki/excerpt posts.
